Before the Beginning

Before kingdoms rose, before temples covered the ancient world, and before humanity recorded its history, stories were carried from one generation to the next.

An ancient tablet buried beneath the earth will lead archaeologist Maya Reed into a mystery that reaches across Mesopotamia, Samaria, and the oldest memories of humanity.

Alongside Professor Elias Ben-Ami and the mysterious Yosef, Maya will discover that some memories were never meant to disappear.
