CHAPTER XXVII
INTERLUDE

THE PRAYER

The chamber fell silent.

Maya stood before the ancient cradle, unable to move.

Then the walls began to glow.

Words appeared across the stone.

Not the language of the Shomrim.

Not the language of the tablets.

A prayer.

Maya read it slowly:

Πάτερ ἡμῶν ὁ ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς,
ἁγιασθήτω τὸ ὄνομά σου·
ἐλθέτω ἡ βασιλεία σου·
γενηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου,
ὡς ἐν οὐρανῷ καὶ ἐπὶ γῆς.

Τὸν ἄρτον ἡμῶν τὸν ἐπιούσιον
δὸς ἡμῖν σήμερον·
καὶ ἄφες ἡμῖν τὰ ὀφειλήματα ἡμῶν,
ὡς καὶ ἡμεῖς ἀφήκαμεν
τοῖς ὀφειλέταις ἡμῶν·
καὶ μὴ εἰσενέγκῃς ἡμᾶς εἰς πειρασμόν,
ἀλλὰ ῥῦσαι ἡμᾶς ἀπὸ τοῦ πονηροῦ.

Maya whispered the meaning:

Our Father who is in the heavens,

let your name be sanctified.

Let your kingdom come.

Let your will be done,

as in heaven, also upon earth.

Give us today our necessary bread.

And forgive us our debts,

as we also have forgiven our debtors.

And do not bring us into testing,

but rescue us from the evil one.

Elias’s voice came from somewhere beyond the chamber.

“Maya…”

She turned.

The words on the wall began disappearing.

One final sentence remained.

Not part of the prayer.

A warning.

THE PRAYER WAS NEVER THE KEY.

Maya stared at the inscription.

Then another word appeared beneath it.

THE NAME IS.

The chamber went completely dark.

And from inside the ancient cradle—

the child began to laugh.
