CHAPTER TWENTY-THREE

THE AWAKENING

The ceiling exploded.

Stone crashed into the chamber.

Maya pulled Jonathan toward her as Elias shielded them from the falling debris.

Adrian shouted:

“MOVE!”

They ran.

Behind them, the ancient prison began collapsing.

A roar shook the city.

Maya looked back.

Something enormous moved through the broken ceiling.

She couldn't see its body.

Only darkness.

And two massive eyes.

Jonathan grabbed her arm.

“Don't look at it.”

“What is that thing?”

“The reason this city was buried.”

They reached the upper chamber.

The people who had been kneeling were now running in every direction.

Their voices echoed through the ruins.

“The seal is broken!”

“The first one has awakened!”

“Get to the surface!”

Maya stopped.

“First one?”

Jonathan looked at her.

“That's what they called it.”

“What did you call it?”

Jonathan's expression darkened.

“A god.”

Elias stared at him.

“You think that thing is a god?”

Jonathan shook his head.

“No.”

He looked toward the darkness.

“I think people called it a god because they were afraid to call it what it really was.”

Maya felt the symbol on her hand burning.

She looked down.

The symbol had changed.

A second mark had appeared beneath it.

Jonathan saw it.

His face went pale.

“No.”

“What?” Maya asked.

Jonathan stepped backward.

“That's impossible.”

Adrian approached.

He looked at Maya's hand.

Then he smiled.

“It's begun.”

Jonathan grabbed him.

“What did you do?”

Adrian didn't resist.

“Nothing.”

Jonathan pushed him away.

“You brought her here knowing this would happen.”

Adrian looked at Maya.

“She was always coming.”

A deep vibration passed through the floor.

Every light in the ancient city went out.

Darkness swallowed the chamber.

Then Maya heard a voice.

Not from the darkness.

From inside her own mind.

“Remember.”

Maya closed her eyes.

Images flooded her mind.

The ancient city.

The first keepers.

The door.

The creature.

Her mother.

Her father.

And herself.

But she wasn't a newborn.

She was standing inside the ancient city.

Thousands of years earlier.

Wearing the same symbol.

Holding the same fragment.

Maya opened her eyes.

“I've been here before.”

Jonathan stared at her.

“What did you see?”

“Me.”

Her voice trembled.

“I saw myself.”

Elias stepped closer.

“How?”

Maya looked toward the ancient doorway.

“I don't think I'm remembering the past.”

She looked at her father.

“I think I'm remembering who I am.”

The city shook violently.

A crack appeared across the floor.

From beneath it came a brilliant light.

Adrian whispered:

“The second city.”

Maya stared at him.

“There's another city?”

Adrian nodded.

“Beneath this one.”

Jonathan stepped forward.

“And that's where the first one came from.”

The crack widened.

A staircase appeared beneath the ancient floor.

Maya looked down.

At the bottom was a doorway.

And carved across it were three words.

THE BEGINNING IS BELOW.

Maya took a breath.

Then stepped toward the stairs.

Elias followed.

Jonathan looked at Adrian.

“If she goes down there, there's no coming back.”

Adrian watched Maya disappear into the darkness.

“There was never going to be.”
