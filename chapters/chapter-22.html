CHAPTER TWENTY-TWO

THE DAUGHTER

Maya couldn't move.

Two enormous eyes stared from the darkness.

They weren't human.

They weren't even alive in any way she understood.

Yet somehow, she felt recognized.

The voice came again.

“Daughter.”

Jonathan pulled against the chains.

“Don't listen to it!”

Maya looked at her father.

“What is it?”

Jonathan's breathing became uneven.

“The first one.”

Elias stepped forward.

“The first what?”

Jonathan looked toward Adrian.

“You brought her here.”

Adrian shook his head.

“She found the way herself.”

Jonathan's eyes hardened.

“You knew this would happen.”

“We knew it had to happen.”

Maya looked between them.

“Someone start explaining.”

The enormous eyes slowly disappeared.

The chamber became silent.

Jonathan stared at his daughter.

“Twenty-three years ago, your mother and I discovered this city.”

Maya listened.

“We thought we had found the remains of an ancient civilization.”

He looked around the chamber.

“We were wrong.”

Elias asked:

“What did you find?”

Jonathan swallowed.

“A civilization that wasn't dead.”

Maya felt a chill.

“The people upstairs?”

Jonathan shook his head.

“They're only descendants.”

Adrian stepped closer.

“Tell her the truth.”

Jonathan glared at him.

“The truth nearly destroyed her once.”

Maya's voice became quiet.

“What are you talking about?”

Jonathan looked directly into her eyes.

“You weren't supposed to survive.”

Maya froze.

“What?”

“When you were born, the symbol appeared on your chest.”

Maya instinctively touched her shirt.

“Your mother wanted to take you and run.”

Jonathan's voice cracked.

“I wanted to protect you.”

“From what?”

Jonathan looked toward the darkness.

“From them.”

Adrian interrupted.

“She was chosen before she was born.”

Maya turned toward him.

“Chosen by who?”

Adrian didn't answer.

Jonathan did.

“By the city.”

The walls began to vibrate.

Ancient symbols lit up one by one.

Maya's hand began glowing.

Jonathan looked terrified.

“It's happening again.”

Elias grabbed Maya's arm.

“What's happening?”

Jonathan shouted:

“The awakening!”

The chains around Jonathan suddenly snapped.

He fell forward.

Maya caught him.

For a moment, father and daughter simply stared at one another.

Jonathan touched her face.

“You grew up.”

Maya's eyes filled with tears.

“I thought you were dead.”

“I wanted to come home.”

“Then why didn't you?”

Jonathan looked toward the ceiling.

“Because if I left, it would follow me.”

A deep rumble shook the chamber.

Adrian looked toward the entrance.

“We have to go.”

Elias nodded.

“Agreed.”

Jonathan suddenly grabbed Maya's wrist.

“No.”

Everyone stopped.

Jonathan stared at the symbol glowing on her hand.

“It's too late.”

Maya looked at him.

“Too late for what?”

Jonathan whispered:

“The door wasn't opened to let something out.”

He looked toward the darkness.

“It was opened to let something in.”

The chamber went completely silent.

Then a voice echoed from everywhere at once.

“Welcome home, daughter.”

Maya looked up.

The ceiling began cracking.

Something enormous was coming through.
