CHAPTER 26

THE FIRST

Maya could not move.

The voice was still inside her mind.

Finally.

She looked into the darkness.

The enormous figure was no longer distant.

It stood directly before them.

Elias raised his weapon.

“Maya, get behind me.”

She didn't move.

Something about the creature felt familiar.

Not threatening.

Familiar.

The keeper stepped between them.

“Lower your weapon.”

Elias didn't listen.

“You said it was the First.”

“It is.”

“Then what exactly is it?”

The keeper looked at Maya.

“The beginning.”

The creature moved.

The darkness itself seemed to bend around it.

Maya finally spoke.

“Why does it know me?”

The keeper remained silent.

“Tell me!” Maya shouted.

The keeper lowered his head.

“Because you were never meant to be separate.”

Maya stared at him.

“What does that mean?”

Before he could answer, the creature raised one enormous hand.

Golden light filled the darkness.

Maya's body lifted from the ground.

“Maya!” Elias shouted.

He reached for her.

But she was already surrounded by light.

Images appeared around her.

The ancient city.

The tablet.

The underground chamber.

The people who had built the city.

Then Maya saw herself.

Not as she was now.

As a child.

Standing beside the ancient tablet.

But she was not alone.

A woman stood behind her.

Maya recognized her immediately.

Her mother.

Maya's eyes filled with tears.

“Mom?”

The image continued.

Her mother placed a hand on the tablet.

The same golden light appeared.

Then she looked directly toward Maya.

“When the time comes,” her mother said, “you must remember.”

The vision disappeared.

Maya dropped to the ground.

Elias caught her.

“Maya.”

She looked at him.

“She knew.”

“Who?”

“My mother.”

The keeper stepped closer.

“Your mother was one of the keepers.”

Maya stood.

“You knew her?”

He nodded.

“She was the last one before you.”

Maya's expression hardened.

“Why didn't she tell me?”

The keeper looked toward the First.

“Because knowing the truth too early would have awakened you.”

“And now?”

“Now there is nothing left to hide.”

A low rumble moved through the darkness.

The First lowered its head.

Its golden eyes met Maya's.

For the first time, Maya understood the voice.

It wasn't speaking to her.

It was speaking through her.

Remember.

Maya closed her eyes.

Images flooded her mind.

Ancient cities.

Forgotten civilizations.

People kneeling beneath a sky filled with fire.

A great light descending from the heavens.

And a group of people carrying the tablet away from it.

Then she saw the truth.

The city had not been built around the creature.

The city had been built around Maya.

Her eyes opened.

“It wasn't protecting them.”

Elias looked at her.

“What?”

Maya stared at the ancient symbols.

“The city wasn't built to keep the First inside.”

The keeper's face went pale.

Maya continued.

“It was built to keep me inside.”

Silence.

Elias slowly lowered his weapon.

“Why?”

Maya looked toward the First.

“Because I'm not its prisoner.”

The creature stepped forward.

The darkness shook.

Maya whispered:

“I'm its key.”

The keeper stepped backward.

“No.”

Maya turned toward him.

“You knew.”

He said nothing.

“You all knew.”

The keeper finally spoke.

“We were afraid of what would happen if you remembered.”

Maya looked at the First.

“And what happens now?”

The keeper looked toward the darkness behind them.

Something was moving there.

Something else.

His voice became barely a whisper.

“Now they know you're awake.”

Elias turned.

“Who?”

The keeper pointed toward the distant darkness.

Thousands of lights appeared.

One after another.

Eyes.

Watching them.

Maya stood beside Elias.

The golden light returned to her eyes.

The First moved behind her.

And for the first time, Maya wasn't afraid.

She looked into the darkness and whispered:

“Let them come.”

The city answered.

Far away, beneath the ancient streets, every bell began to ring.

The awakening was complete.
