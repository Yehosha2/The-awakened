CHAPTER 14

THE VOICE

Maya took the first step.

Elias caught her wrist.

“Don’t.”

She looked back at him.

“You heard him.”

“I heard a voice.”

“It was my father.”

“You haven’t heard your father’s voice in eleven years.”

Maya stared at the darkness below.

“Exactly.”

She pulled free.

Then descended.

Elias cursed under his breath and followed.

The staircase seemed to have no end.

Every step was carved with a name.

Maya recognized some.

Kings.

Priests.

Generals.

But others were unfamiliar.

And beneath every name was the same symbol:

✦

Halfway down, Maya stopped.

One name had been carved recently.

The stone around it was still clean.

No dust.

No erosion.

No age.

She raised her flashlight.

JONATHAN REED

Her father’s name.

Maya’s breathing stopped.

Elias stared at the inscription.

“He was here.”

Maya touched the letters.

“They said he disappeared.”

“He did.”

“No.”

She looked at him.

“He didn’t disappear.”

Her voice trembled.

“He came here.”

A metallic sound echoed from below.

CLANG.

They froze.

Another.

CLANG.

Closer.

Elias whispered, “Someone is coming.”

Maya turned toward the darkness above.

Nothing.

The sound came again.

But it wasn’t coming from above.

It was coming from below them.

Elias raised his weapon.

“Keep moving.”

They descended faster.

At the bottom stood a narrow corridor.

At the end was a door.

And scratched across it were four words:

DO NOT REMEMBER HIM.

Maya stared.

“Who?”

Elias didn’t answer.

The fragment in Maya’s hand suddenly went cold.

Then—

a man’s voice whispered directly beside her ear.

“Because I am not your father.”

Maya spun around.

Nothing.

Elias grabbed her shoulders.

“What happened?”

Before she could answer, the door opened.

A figure stood inside.

An old man.

Thin.

Exhausted.

His hair was almost completely white.

Maya dropped the fragment.

“Dad?”

The man looked at her.

His eyes filled with tears.

But he didn’t move.

“Maya…”

She stepped forward.

He raised his hand.

“Stay back.”

“It’s me.”

“I know.”

“Dad, I’ve been looking for you.”

His face twisted with fear.

“You shouldn’t have found me.”

Elias stepped forward.

“Who are you?”

The old man looked at him.

Then at the glowing fragment on the floor.

His expression changed completely.

“Where did you get that?”

Maya picked it up.

“From the chamber.”

Her father backed away.

“No.”

“What?”

“You must destroy it.”

Elias frowned.

“Why?”

Her father whispered:

“Because it isn’t a key.”

The lights in the corridor went out.

His voice became barely audible.

“It is a signal.”

Something moved behind him.

Maya stared into the darkness.

“Dad…”

He looked over his shoulder.

His face went white.

Then he whispered:

“It’s awake.”

A massive sound rolled through the underground city.

Not a roar.

Not thunder.

A heartbeat.

THUMP.

The walls shook.

THUMP.

Dust fell from the ceiling.

THUMP.

Maya looked at her father.

He grabbed her arm.

“Run.”

Behind him, something opened its eyes.
