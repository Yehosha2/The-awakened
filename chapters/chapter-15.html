CHAPTER 15

THE EYES IN THE DARK

Maya didn’t run.

She couldn’t.

Her father’s hand was locked around her wrist, but she stood frozen, staring past him.

Two enormous eyes had opened in the darkness.

They weren’t human.

And they were watching her.

“Dad…” Maya whispered.

Jonathan pulled her toward the corridor.

“Don’t look at it.”

“What is that?”

“Something that was never supposed to wake.”

Elias backed toward them, weapon raised.

“How do we stop it?”

Jonathan looked at him.

“We don’t.”

THUMP.

The corridor shook.

Stone cracked beneath their feet.

Jonathan grabbed the glowing fragment from Maya’s hand.

The moment his fingers touched it, a blinding flash erupted through the chamber.

Jonathan screamed.

The fragment fell.

Maya caught him before he hit the ground.

His hand was burned.

But he barely noticed.

He stared at her.

“I’ve spent eleven years keeping it asleep.”

Maya’s voice trembled.

“Why didn’t you come home?”

Jonathan looked away.

“Because it followed me.”

A scraping sound came from behind the doorway.

Elias whispered:

“What followed you?”

Jonathan swallowed.

“Not what.”

He looked toward the darkness.

“Who.”

The enormous eyes moved closer.

A shape slowly emerged.

Tall.

Far too tall to be human.

Its body was covered in ancient markings that shifted across its skin like living writing.

Maya stepped backward.

The creature stopped.

It looked directly at her.

Then it spoke.

Not with one voice.

With hundreds.

“Maya Reed.”

Her blood ran cold.

Elias raised his weapon.

The creature turned toward him.

“Elias.”

Elias froze.

Jonathan whispered:

“It knows both of you.”

Maya stared at her father.

“How?”

Jonathan’s expression changed.

“Because you were never supposed to meet.”

The creature took one step forward.

The floor shattered beneath its foot.

Maya grabbed Elias.

“Run!”

They turned.

The corridor behind them was gone.

A wall of stone had sealed their escape.

Jonathan looked at the creature.

Then at Maya.

For the first time since she had found him, her father smiled.

Not because he was happy.

Because he had made a decision.

“Maya.”

“What?”

“Remember what I told you when you were a child.”

She shook her head.

“I don’t remember.”

Jonathan’s eyes filled with tears.

“You will.”

The creature moved.

Jonathan stepped between it and his daughter.

Elias shouted:

“Jonathan!”

Jonathan looked back one final time.

“Before the beginning… there was a choice.”

The darkness swallowed him.

Maya screamed.

The fragment on the floor began to glow.

Not blue.

Not white.

Red.

And somewhere beneath the city, thousands of ancient voices awakened at once.

They all spoke the same word.

“RUN.”
