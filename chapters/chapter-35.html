Chapter XXXV

The First Memory

The walls began closing.

Stone thundered against stone.

Maya reached for the other woman.

“Wait!”

The woman grabbed her hand.

“Don’t let go.”

The chamber collapsed behind them.

Maya screamed as the floor disappeared.

They fell.

Not down.

Through.

For a moment there was no stone, no darkness, no sound.

Only memories.

Thousands of them.

Maya saw cities rise from empty plains.

Ships crossing oceans that no longer existed.

Kings kneeling before nameless figures.

Children being born beneath unfamiliar stars.

Then she saw the mountain.

But it was different.

No ruins.

No temple.

No Shomrim.

Only a vast green landscape beneath a bright sky.

The other Maya stood beside her.

“This is where it began.”

Maya stared.

“Where are we?”

“Before the mountain had a name.”

A group of people appeared in the distance.

They were carrying stones.

Building something.

Maya recognized the symbol carved into each stone.

The same symbol from her pendant.

“Who are they?”

“The first Keepers.”

“Shomrim?”

“Before they called themselves that.”

Maya watched as they built a circular chamber.

At its center they placed a black stone.

The same stone she had seen in the cradle.

Her stomach tightened.

“What is it?”

The other Maya looked at her.

“A memory.”

“Of what?”

“Everything.”

✦ ✦ ✦

The black stone began glowing.

The people fell to their knees.

A voice came from the stone.

“Choose.”

Maya stepped closer.

“Choose what?”

The other Maya didn’t answer.

The stone showed images.

War.

Death.

Floods.

Fire.

Civilizations disappearing.

Then humanity starting again.

Again.

And again.

Maya understood.

“This has happened before.”

“Yes.”

“How many times?”

The other Maya looked toward the horizon.

“Enough that nobody remembers the first.”

Maya felt cold.

“What happens when they forget?”

“The world resets.”

“And me?”

“You remember.”

“Why?”

The other Maya smiled sadly.

“Because someone has to.”

✦ ✦ ✦

A distant voice interrupted them.

“Maya!”

She turned.

Elias was running toward them.

Behind him came her mother.

Both looked terrified.

The other Maya stepped forward.

“You shouldn’t have followed us.”

Elias raised the ancient blade.

“I promised I would stop you.”

The other Maya smiled.

“You’ve made that promise before.”

Elias looked at Maya.

“Don’t believe her.”

Maya stared at him.

“Then tell me the truth.”

He lowered the blade.

“The first Maya wasn’t human.”

Silence.

Maya looked at the other woman.

She nodded.

“I was created to remember.”

Maya’s mother stepped forward.

“And you were created to choose.”

Maya’s breath caught.

“Choose what?”

Her mother looked toward the black stone.

“Whether this world continues…”

The stone began cracking.

“…or begins again.”

✦ ✦ ✦

A deafening sound shook the sky.

The black stone split open.

Inside was a single glowing symbol.

Maya recognized it immediately.

It was the same mark she had carried since childhood.

The other Maya whispered:

“The choice has returned.”

The sky darkened.

And somewhere beyond the horizon—

the first sun began to rise.
