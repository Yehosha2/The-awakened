CHAPTER TWENTY

THE ONES WHO REMEMBER

Maya stared at the kneeling figures.

Dozens of people.

Men and women.

Young and old.

Every one of them wearing the same symbol.

The same symbol that had appeared on her hand.

None of them moved.

None of them spoke.

They simply knelt.

Waiting.

Elias stepped in front of Maya.

“Stay behind me.”

The stranger laughed quietly.

“You still don't understand.”

Elias tightened his grip on the fragment.

“Understand what?”

The stranger looked at Maya.

“She isn't here because of the fragment.”

Maya felt a chill move through her.

“Then why am I here?”

The stranger slowly walked toward her.

“Because the city called you.”

Maya looked around.

“The city?”

“It has been waiting beneath the earth for thousands of years.”

“Waiting for me?”

“Waiting for the one who could hear it.”

Maya looked at her hand.

The symbol was glowing again.

Faintly.

Like a heartbeat.

She closed her fingers.

“Who are you?”

The stranger stopped.

“My name is Adrian.”

Elias stepped forward.

“And these people?”

Adrian turned toward them.

“They are descendants of the first keepers.”

Maya frowned.

“Keepers of what?”

Adrian looked toward the enormous chamber behind them.

“The door.”

Silence filled the ruins.

Maya remembered her father's voice.

She is the only one who can open the door.

Her stomach tightened.

“My father knew about you.”

Adrian's expression changed.

“Your father knew more than you realize.”

“Where is he?”

Adrian didn't answer.

“Where is my father?” Maya demanded.

Adrian looked down.

“He came here twenty-three years ago.”

Maya froze.

Twenty-three years.

The same year she was born.

“What happened to him?”

Adrian raised his eyes.

“He opened the first door.”

Elias looked at Maya.

“The first?”

Adrian nodded.

“There is more than one.”

A deep sound rolled through the chamber.

Everyone looked upward.

Dust fell from the ceiling.

The ancient stones began moving.

One of the kneeling figures suddenly stood.

“It's beginning.”

Another person whispered:

“The city is waking.”

Maya stepped backward.

“What is happening?”

Adrian looked directly at her.

“You woke it.”

The ground beneath Maya's feet began glowing.

A circle of ancient symbols appeared around her.

Elias reached for her.

“Maya!”

He tried to cross the circle.

A force threw him backward.

“Elias!”

Maya rushed toward him.

The symbols flashed.

And suddenly the entire chamber disappeared.

Maya stood somewhere else.

A vast black sky stretched above her.

Stars filled the darkness.

But they were moving.

Not across the sky.

They were moving toward her.

A figure appeared in the distance.

Maya stopped breathing.

She knew that face.

“Dad?”

The figure turned.

Jonathan Reed stood beneath the ancient stars.

Older.

Exhausted.

But alive.

He looked directly at her.

“Maya.”

She ran toward him.

“Dad!”

Jonathan raised his hand.

“Don't come any closer.”

Maya stopped.

“Where are you?”

Jonathan looked behind him.

Something enormous moved in the darkness.

His face filled with fear.

“You have to listen to me.”

“What happened to you?”

Jonathan shook his head.

“I didn't disappear.”

He looked at Maya's glowing hand.

“I was trapped.”

Maya's voice trembled.

“By what?”

Jonathan looked into the darkness.

“By the thing your mother and I were trying to keep asleep.”

The stars suddenly went dark.

Jonathan whispered:

“And now it knows your name.”

A massive eye opened behind him.

Maya screamed.

Jonathan shouted:

“RUN!”

The vision shattered.

Maya slammed onto the stone floor.

Elias was beside her instantly.

“Maya! Maya, look at me!”

She opened her eyes.

The chamber was still there.

But something had changed.

Every person in the room was standing.

Adrian looked toward the ceiling.

Then the first bell rang.

Once.

Twice.

Then hundreds of bells began ringing throughout the ancient city.

Maya slowly stood.

“What does that mean?”

Adrian looked at her.

For the first time, he looked afraid.

“It means the second door has opened.”

Maya's eyes widened.

“And what's behind it?”

Adrian stared into the darkness.

“Your father's prison.”

Another bell rang.

This time, the entire city answered.

And somewhere beneath them, something began to laugh.
