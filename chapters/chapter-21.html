CHAPTER TWENTY-ONE

THE PRISON

The laughter continued.

Low.

Deep.

Almost human.

Maya stood completely still.

Elias looked toward the floor.

“Tell me that was just an echo.”

Adrian shook his head.

“It wasn't.”

The bells continued ringing throughout the ancient city.

One after another.

Calling something awake.

Maya looked at Adrian.

“Take me to my father.”

Adrian didn't move.

“You don't understand what you're asking.”

“I understand perfectly.”

Maya stepped closer.

“He's alive.”

“Yes.”

“Then I'm going to him.”

Elias moved beside her.

“We're going.”

Adrian studied them both.

Finally, he turned toward the ruins.

“Then stay close.”

They followed him through a narrow passage.

The walls were covered with ancient carvings.

Maya stopped.

One of the carvings showed a woman standing before a massive door.

Above her head was the same symbol that appeared on Maya's hand.

“That's me.”

Adrian looked back.

“Not exactly.”

Maya frowned.

“What does that mean?”

“It means the symbol existed long before you did.”

Elias examined the wall.

“Who is the woman?”

Adrian answered quietly.

“The first awakened.”

Maya touched the carving.

The instant her fingers touched the stone, the symbol on her hand burned.

She pulled away.

“It reacted to me.”

Adrian nodded.

“The city recognizes blood.”

Maya stared at him.

“My blood?”

“Your father's.”

The passage suddenly shook.

Stone fell from the ceiling.

Elias grabbed Maya.

“Move!”

They ran.

Behind them came a sound like something enormous dragging itself across stone.

Adrian reached a massive doorway.

He placed both hands against it.

Nothing happened.

He looked at Maya.

“Your turn.”

Maya hesitated.

“What do I do?”

“Touch the door.”

She slowly raised her hand.

Her fingers touched the ancient stone.

The entire doorway illuminated.

Symbols appeared across its surface.

Then the stone began opening.

Elias stared.

“You just opened it.”

Maya looked at Adrian.

“Where does it lead?”

Adrian's face became pale.

“Below.”

The doorway revealed a staircase descending into darkness.

Maya looked down.

She could hear breathing.

Slow.

Heavy.

Alive.

Then a voice rose from the darkness.

“Maya…”

She froze.

She knew that voice.

“Dad?”

Elias grabbed her arm.

“Wait.”

But Maya pulled free.

“That's him.”

She stepped onto the first stair.

Then the second.

Then the third.

Adrian followed.

Elias came behind him.

The darkness swallowed them.

At the bottom of the staircase, Maya saw a chamber.

Chains covered the walls.

Ancient symbols surrounded a single figure.

Maya stopped.

“Dad…”

Jonathan Reed lifted his head.

His face was older than the vision.

His hair was streaked with gray.

But his eyes were unmistakable.

“Maya.”

She ran toward him.

Jonathan suddenly shouted:

“STOP!”

Maya froze.

Jonathan looked at the symbol on her hand.

Fear filled his eyes.

“It followed you.”

Maya whispered:

“What followed me?”

Jonathan looked past her.

His expression changed.

“The thing I've been keeping asleep.”

A shadow moved behind Maya.

Elias turned.

“Maya…”

She slowly looked back.

Nothing was there.

Then Jonathan screamed:

“DON'T LOOK AT IT!”

Maya turned anyway.

Two enormous eyes opened in the darkness.

And a voice whispered:

“Daughter.”
