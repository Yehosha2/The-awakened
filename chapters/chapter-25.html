CHAPTER 25

THE AWAKENING

The city was silent.

Not the peaceful silence of night.

This silence felt alive.

Maya stood at the edge of the ancient chamber, staring into the darkness below.

Elias stood beside her.

Neither of them spoke.

The inscription on the wall had stopped glowing.

But Maya could still feel its presence.

Something had changed.

Something inside the city.

Something inside her.

“Maya,” Elias whispered.

She turned toward him.

“What?”

He looked frightened.

“Listen.”

Maya froze.

At first, she heard nothing.

Then she heard it.

A heartbeat.

Slow.

Deep.

Impossible.

Thump.

The stone beneath their feet trembled.

Thump.

Dust fell from the ceiling.

Thump.

The ancient walls began to glow.

Elias stepped backward.

“That isn't the city.”

Maya stared into the darkness.

“No.”

Another tremor shook the chamber.

“It's underneath it.”

A crack appeared across the stone floor.

Maya watched as a faint golden light escaped from beneath it.

The light spread through the cracks like veins.

Then the entire chamber illuminated.

Symbols appeared across the walls.

Thousands of them.

Ancient symbols.

Warnings.

Names.

Stories.

And one symbol that Maya recognized immediately.

The same symbol that had appeared in her dreams.

The same symbol that had been carved into the tablet.

The same symbol that had followed her since the beginning.

Elias stared at it.

“What is that?”

Maya touched the symbol.

The moment her fingers made contact, the chamber disappeared.

She was standing somewhere else.

A vast darkness surrounded her.

Stars filled the distance.

Thousands of them.

Millions.

And beneath them stood an enormous figure.

Maya couldn't see its face.

Only its eyes.

Two burning lights stared directly at her.

“Who are you?” Maya asked.

The figure did not answer.

Instead, the darkness behind it began to move.

Something enormous was approaching.

The figure turned.

For the first time, Maya heard its voice.

“They were never supposed to find you.”

Maya's heart stopped.

“Who?”

The figure looked back at her.

“The ones who created the city.”

The stars began disappearing.

One by one.

Then the darkness swallowed everything.

Maya screamed.

Her eyes opened.

She was back inside the chamber.

Elias was holding her shoulders.

“Maya!”

She looked at him.

“I saw them.”

“Saw who?”

Maya looked toward the glowing symbol.

“The creators.”

Elias went silent.

Then another voice came from the darkness.

“No.”

They both turned.

An old man stepped from the shadows.

His clothes were covered in dust.

His face was marked with ancient scars.

Elias reached for the weapon at his side.

“Who are you?”

The old man looked at Maya.

His eyes widened.

“You finally came.”

Maya stepped forward.

“You know me?”

The old man nodded.

“I have been waiting for you for a very long time.”

“How long?”

He looked toward the ancient walls.

“Longer than this city has existed.”

Elias lowered his weapon slightly.

“That's impossible.”

The old man smiled.

“So is she.”

Maya stared at him.

“What does that mean?”

The old man pointed toward her eyes.

“You are not the first.”

Maya's expression changed.

“The first what?”

The old man stepped closer.

“The first awakened.”

The chamber shook violently.

Stone collapsed from the ceiling.

The old man looked toward the darkness.

His expression changed.

“They're coming.”

Elias raised his weapon.

“Who?”

The old man looked directly at Maya.

“The ones who buried the truth.”

Another heartbeat echoed through the city.

Thump.

The lights went out.

For several seconds, there was complete darkness.

Then Maya heard something behind her.

A whisper.

Her own voice.

“Wake up.”

Maya turned.

Two golden eyes opened in the darkness.

And the city began to rise.
