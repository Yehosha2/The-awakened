CHAPTER 24

THE GATE

The city had stopped moving.

For the first time since the ancient chamber had awakened, everything was still.

Maya stood in the center of the ruined hall.

Elias remained beside her, staring at the enormous doorway that had appeared in the wall.

Neither of them spoke.

The doorway was unlike anything they had seen before.

It was perfectly circular.

Ancient symbols covered its surface.

At its center was the same symbol Maya had seen on the tablet.

Her symbol.

Elias stepped closer.

“That wasn't there before.”

Maya shook her head.

“No.”

She reached toward the door.

Elias grabbed her arm.

“Wait.”

Maya looked at him.

“Why?”

“Because every time you touch something in this place, something terrible happens.”

Maya almost smiled.

“That's not entirely true.”

“Maya.”

She looked back at the doorway.

“We came here for answers.”

Elias released her arm.

“Then let's get them together.”

Maya placed her hand against the symbol.

The chamber went silent.

Then the symbol began to glow.

Golden light spread beneath Maya's fingers.

The entire doorway trembled.

A deep sound echoed through the chamber.

Not a voice.

Something older.

The door began to open.

Slowly.

Stone moved against stone.

A cold wind rushed through the chamber.

Maya stepped backward.

Elias raised his arm to protect his face.

Then the door opened completely.

Beyond it was darkness.

Not ordinary darkness.

There was no floor.

No ceiling.

No walls.

Only an endless black space filled with tiny points of light.

Maya stared into it.

“What is that?”

Elias slowly lowered his arm.

“I don't know.”

A whisper came from beyond the doorway.

“Maya.”

She froze.

Elias looked at her.

“You heard that too?”

Maya nodded.

The voice came again.

“Maya.”

She stepped toward the doorway.

Elias followed.

“Don't go alone.”

They crossed the threshold.

The moment Maya stepped through, the city disappeared behind them.

Elias turned around.

The doorway was gone.

“Maya.”

She looked around.

They were standing beneath a sky filled with stars.

But there was something wrong with them.

The stars were moving.

Slowly.

As if the entire universe were turning around them.

Maya looked down.

There was no ground beneath her feet.

Yet she was standing.

Elias stared into the distance.

“Where are we?”

Maya didn't answer.

Something was approaching.

A massive shape moved between the stars.

At first it looked like a mountain.

Then it moved.

Elias stepped backward.

“That's not a mountain.”

Maya watched the enormous figure emerge from the darkness.

Its body seemed to stretch across the sky.

Its eyes burned with golden light.

The same golden light that had appeared in Maya's eyes.

The figure stopped.

Then it spoke.

“You should never have opened the gate.”

Maya's heart began pounding.

“Who are you?”

The figure remained silent.

Then another voice answered from behind them.

“The question is not who it is.”

Maya turned.

A man stood in the darkness.

He wore ancient robes covered in symbols.

Elias moved in front of Maya.

“Who are you?”

The man looked at Maya.

“I am the last keeper.”

Maya stepped around Elias.

“Keeper of what?”

The man's expression became serious.

“The truth.”

Maya stared at him.

“Then tell me the truth.”

The keeper looked toward the enormous figure in the distance.

“The city was never built to protect humanity.”

Elias frowned.

“Then what was it built for?”

The keeper looked directly into Maya's eyes.

“To keep something inside.”

Silence filled the space.

Maya felt the ground beneath her disappear.

“The monster.”

The keeper nodded.

“You called it a monster because you did not know what else to call it.”

Maya looked toward the enormous figure.

“Then what is it?”

The keeper whispered:

“The first.”

A violent tremor shook the darkness.

The stars began disappearing.

One after another.

The enormous figure opened its eyes.

The keeper turned toward Maya.

“You have awakened it.”

Maya looked at her hands.

Golden light was spreading across her skin.

Elias grabbed her hand.

“Maya, what's happening?”

She looked at him.

Her eyes were glowing.

“I don't know.”

The keeper stepped backward.

For the first time, fear appeared on his face.

“No.”

Maya looked at him.

“What?”

He stared at her eyes.

“It isn't awakening because you opened the gate.”

Maya's breathing stopped.

The keeper whispered:

“It is awakening because you are.”

A deafening sound exploded through the darkness.

The stars vanished.

The enormous figure moved closer.

And Maya heard a voice inside her mind.

Finally.

She looked toward the darkness.

Something had begun.

And there was no going back.
