CHAPTER 16

THE MONSTER WITHIN

The word RUN continued to echo through the underground city.

Maya couldn’t move.

Her father was gone.

The creature was gone.

Only the red glow of the fragment remained.

Elias grabbed her hand.

“Maya. We have to go.”

She didn’t respond.

“Maya!”

She finally looked at him.

“Where?”

Elias turned toward the sealed corridor.

“I don’t know.”

A faint whisper came from behind them.

Maya froze.

“Did you hear that?”

Elias nodded.

The whisper came again.

“Maya…”

She turned.

Nothing.

Then the fragment pulsed.

THUMP.

Maya gasped.

Pain shot through her chest.

She dropped to her knees.

Elias caught her.

“What is happening?”

“I don’t know!”

Her vision blurred.

For an instant, she wasn’t underground anymore.

She was standing in a field.

A field beneath a black sky.

Thousands of people stood around her.

Silent.

Watching.

At the center was a young man dressed in white.

His face was hidden by light.

He raised his hand.

And everyone fell to their knees.

Then Maya heard a voice.

“You remember now.”

She looked down.

Her hands were covered in blood.

She screamed.

The vision vanished.

Maya opened her eyes.

Elias was staring at her.

“What did you see?”

She couldn’t answer.

Something had changed.

The fragment was no longer lying on the ground.

It was floating.

Directly in front of her.

Elias slowly stepped backward.

“Maya…”

“What?”

“Look at your hand.”

She looked down.

A symbol had appeared on her palm.

The same symbol carved throughout the ancient city.

Three lines inside a circle.

But now there was something different.

A fourth line was forming.

Slowly.

Like an invisible hand was drawing it beneath her skin.

Maya cried out.

The lights throughout the city suddenly exploded.

Darkness returned.

Then—

two enormous eyes appeared at the end of the corridor.

The creature had returned.

But this time it didn’t move toward them.

It was afraid.

Elias whispered:

“Why is it backing away?”

Maya stared at the creature.

Then she understood.

It wasn’t looking at Elias.

It wasn’t looking at the fragment.

It was looking at her.

The creature lowered its head.

Almost like it was bowing.

Maya’s voice trembled.

“Elias…”

“What?”

“I think…”

She looked at the symbol burning into her palm.

“…it’s afraid of me.”

A voice answered from the darkness.

Not the creature.

Her father’s voice.

“No, Maya.”

A pause.

Then:

“It’s afraid of what is waking up inside you.”

The creature suddenly screamed.

The sound shook the entire underground city.

Maya clutched her chest.

Something inside her answered.

THUMP.

The creature retreated into the darkness.

Elias stared at Maya in horror.

“What are you?”

Maya looked at her glowing hand.

“I don’t know.”

Then the ancient doors behind them slammed shut.

And a final inscription appeared across the stone:

THE MONSTER WAS NEVER BENEATH THE CITY.

Maya read the words.

Her blood ran cold.

The final line appeared.

IT WAS WITHIN.
