CHAPTER 12

Seek and find, knock and the door with be opened

The door opened without a sound.

Maya stood perfectly still.

Beyond it was not another chamber.

It was a vast underground city.

Elias raised his flashlight, but the beam disappeared into the darkness before reaching the opposite wall.

Stone towers rose from the cavern floor.

Bridges crossed enormous gaps.

Carved pillars stretched toward a ceiling that could barely be seen.

And everywhere—

there were lights.

Tiny points of blue-white fire burned inside ancient bowls along the walls.

Maya whispered, “This city is intact.”

Elias stepped beside her.

“No.”

She looked at him.

“What?”

He pointed toward the floor.

There were footprints.

Fresh ones.

Maya’s heart tightened.

Someone had been here.

Recently.

Elias slowly drew his weapon.

“Stay behind me.”

They entered.

Their footsteps echoed through the enormous chamber.

Maya noticed something strange.

The carvings on the walls weren’t depicting gods.

They showed people.

Thousands of them.

Men.

Women.

Children.

All looking toward the same figure.

A man standing beneath a brilliant star.

Maya moved closer.

There was writing beneath the image.

She recognized the first line.

Ancient Hebrew.

But the second line was something older.

Something she had never seen before.

Elias studied it.

“What does it say?”

Maya shook her head.

“I don’t know.”

Then the fragment in her hand began glowing.

The ancient letters changed.

One by one, they became readable.

Maya’s breath caught.

“It says…”

She swallowed.

“When the memory returns, the sleeping one will awaken.”

Elias looked around.

“Sleeping one?”

A sound echoed through the city.

A bell.

Once.

Twice.

Then three times.

The blue lights along the walls suddenly ignited.

The entire city came alive.

Maya stepped backward.

“Elias…”

“I know.”

“No. Look.”

At the far end of the city stood a massive stone monument.

It hadn’t been there moments before.

Or perhaps the darkness had hidden it.

A figure was carved into it.

The same man from the doorway.

But this time his face was visible.

Maya stared.

She knew that face.

Not because she had seen it in a photograph.

Not because she had seen it in a painting.

She knew it from somewhere deeper.

Somewhere she could not explain.

Elias whispered:

“Yeshua.”

Maya felt tears forming in her eyes.

Then the figure’s eyes opened.

Stone cracked.

The monument moved.

Elias grabbed Maya and pulled her backward.

A deep voice filled the underground city.

Not angry.

Not threatening.

Almost sorrowful.

“You came too early.”

Maya froze.

“Who are you?”

Silence.

Then the voice answered.

“I am not the one you seek.”

The ground beneath them shook.

A second voice whispered from somewhere behind them.

This one was human.

And terrified.

“Run.”

Maya turned.

A man stood at the entrance to the city.

He was dressed in black.

His face was familiar.

Maya had seen him before.

At the excavation.

At the monastery.

And once—

in her dreams.

Elias raised his weapon.

The stranger smiled.

“You finally found the door.”

Maya tightened her grip around the glowing fragment.

The stranger looked directly at it.

Then his smile disappeared.

“You have no idea what you’re carrying.”

The city went dark.

And something enormous moved beneath their feet.
