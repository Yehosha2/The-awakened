Choices and Sacrifice CHAPTER XXXVII AWAKE The world began to wake. Maya felt it beneath her feet. The ancient grass moved without wind. The mountains trembled. Far across the horizon, shadows rose from the earth. Elias looked at Maya. “What did you do?” “I remembered.” “That isn’t an answer.” “It is the only answer.” Her mother stepped beside them. “The cycle has started.” Maya looked toward the black stone. “No.” She reached for it. “The cycle is ending.” The stone cracked. Light poured through the fracture. The entire sky turned white. Then Maya heard thousands of voices. The forgotten. The dead. The people whose names had disappeared from history. They spoke together. “Remember us.” Maya closed her eyes. “I do.” The voices became silent. Then something enormous moved beyond the mountains. Elias raised the ancient blade. “They’re coming.” Maya opened her eyes. “Who?” The first Keeper appeared on the horizon. Behind him stood an army of Shomrim. But they weren’t alone. Something followed them. Tall figures covered in black stone. The same beings Maya had seen in the visions. The original guardians. Her mother whispered: “They were never protecting humanity.” Maya looked at her. “What were they protecting?” Her mother answered: “The cycle.” The first Keeper raised his staff. “Stop her!” The Shomrim charged. Elias stepped forward. Maya grabbed his arm. “Don’t.” He looked at her. “Why?” “Because this isn’t a battle.” “What is it?” “A choice.” Maya walked toward the army. The ground beneath her feet illuminated. The ancient symbol appeared again. The Shomrim stopped. The first Keeper stared at her. “You remember.” “Yes.” “You know what happens if you break the cycle.” “Yes.” “Then you know the cost.” Maya looked toward the sky. “I do.” The Keeper lowered his staff. “What will you sacrifice?” Maya turned toward Elias. Then her mother. Then the black stone. Finally, she looked at herself. “Everything I thought I was.” The black stone exploded. A wave of light swept across the world. The Shomrim fell to their knees. The ancient guardians disappeared. The mountains stopped shaking. The sky cleared. For the first time in thousands of years— there was silence. Elias walked toward Maya. “Is it over?” Maya looked toward the horizon. “No.” “Then what?” She pointed toward the sky. A single door had appeared among the stars. It was enormous. Ancient. And slowly opening. The first Keeper whispered: “That door was never meant to open.” Maya looked at him. “Then why is it opening?” The Keeper’s face went pale. “Because something is coming through.” A shadow appeared behind the door. Maya recognized it immediately. The same presence from her first memory. The one that had entered the black stone. The one that had been waiting. Maya whispered: “I remember you.” The shadow answered: “And now I remember you.” The door opened wider. And the entire sky began to fall.
