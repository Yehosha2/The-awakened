CHAPTER XXVIII

THE SKY REMEMBERS

𐤌𐤉𐤀

The star vanished.

Maya stared upward.

For one impossible second, everything was silent.

Then another star disappeared.

Then another.

One by one, the heavens were going dark.

Elias grabbed her hand.

“We have to leave.”

Maya couldn’t move.

“Where?”

“Anywhere but here.”

A third star vanished.

The darkness between the stars began forming a shape.

Not a constellation.

A face.

Maya felt something inside her recognize it.

“No…”

Elias looked up.

His expression collapsed.

“What?”

“That face.”

“What face?”

Maya pointed.

“The one from my memory.”

The ancient figure.

The one that had told her:

You.

The darkness opened its eyes.

Every Shomrim member still inside the mountain began screaming.

Their voices echoed through the chambers below.

Then the old man’s voice rose above them.

“Close the gate!”

Maya looked at Elias.

“What gate?”

He didn’t answer.

The ground split open.

A massive circular doorway emerged beneath their feet.

Symbols covered its surface.

Maya recognized one.

Her symbol.

Then another.

SHOMRIM.

Then a third.

MAYA.

She stepped backward.

“Why is my name on the gate?”

Elias whispered:

“Because you are the key.”

The gate began turning.

Stone ground against stone.

Something on the other side breathed.

A huge breath.

The entire mountain inhaled.

Maya’s pendant lifted from the ground and floated before her.

The silver casing opened.

Inside, the ancient stone began glowing.

A voice whispered:

“Remember.”

Maya closed her eyes.

Memories came rushing back.

Her mother.

The temple.

The Shomrim.

Elias.

The child.

And then—

herself.

Standing before the gate thousands of years ago.

Not as Maya.

Not as a child.

As the woman from the darkness.

The first memory.

Maya opened her eyes.

“I’ve been here before.”

Elias stared at her.

“Yes.”

“How long ago?”

He couldn’t answer.

Maya stepped toward the gate.

The symbols began lighting beneath her feet.

One after another.

The gate stopped turning.

A voice spoke from the other side.

“You have remembered enough.”

Maya whispered:

“Who are you?”

The answer came immediately.

“The one you created.”

Elias grabbed her.

“No!”

The gate opened.

Darkness poured through.

Elias was thrown backward.

Maya remained standing.

The darkness surrounded her but did not touch her.

Instead, it bowed.

Every Shomrim member fell silent.

The old man entered the chamber.

He looked at Maya.

Then at the open gate.

Tears appeared in his eyes.

“The prophecy was wrong.”

Maya turned.

“What prophecy?”

The old man whispered:

“We thought you were the one who would awaken it.”

He looked toward the darkness.

“We were wrong.”

Maya’s voice hardened.

“Then what am I?”

The old man lowered his head.

“You are the one who can command it.”

Behind Maya, something enormous emerged from the gate.

Elias stared in horror.

“Maya…”

She turned.

The creature lowered itself before her.

And for the first time—

it spoke her name correctly.

Not Maya.

Not the forgotten name.

Something older.

Something that made the mountain tremble.

“Māyā.”

Maya’s eyes widened.

The word echoed through the chamber.

And every ancient tablet hidden beneath the mountain began to glow.

The Shomrim screamed one final warning:

“SHE HAS REMEMBERED.”

Then the gate slammed shut.

And the world above them went completely dark.
