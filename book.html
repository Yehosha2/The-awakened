Chapter One

The Tablet

Maya Reed had spent eleven years searching for things that no longer existed. Cities buried beneath sand. Names worn from stone. Civilizations that history had reduced to fragments and footnotes.

She had learned long ago that the dead were rarely silent.

Sometimes they whispered through inscriptions. Sometimes through ruins. And sometimes, if the stories were true, they left something behind.

✦ ✦ ✦

The tablet was never supposed to have been found.

Yet there it was.

Waiting beneath the darkness.

Chapter Two

The Eyes in the Dark

Elias stood at the entrance to the chamber, staring into a darkness that seemed deeper than the room itself.

Maya slowly raised the light.

Something moved beyond it.

Neither of them spoke.

Then two pale reflections appeared in the darkness.

Eyes.

✦ ✦ ✦

Elias whispered one word.

“Run.”

Chapter Three

The Ancient Voice

The chamber became completely silent.

Then Maya heard something that should not have been possible.

A voice.

Not from Elias. Not from the darkness.

From somewhere beneath them.

✦ ✦ ✦

The tablet began to glow.

<
Chapter Four

The Awakening

Maya reached toward the tablet.

The instant her fingers touched the ancient surface, the room disappeared.

For one impossible moment, she saw a world that existed before the world she knew.

Then she heard her own name.

✦ ✦ ✦

Maya opened her eyes.

Something inside her had awakened.

Chapter Five

No Man's Land

The map led them beyond every road marked on modern charts.

Elias knew they were crossing a boundary long before the instruments stopped working.

There were no birds.

No wind.

Only the feeling that something was watching them.

Chapter Six

The Descent

The stairs disappeared into darkness.

Maya counted every step.

Forty.

Fifty.

Then she stopped counting.

✦ ✦ ✦

Something was coming up the stairs.

Chapter Seven

The Chamber

At the bottom stood a doorway carved into the stone.

There were symbols around its frame that neither Maya nor Elias recognized.

Yet Maya somehow understood what they meant.

✦ ✦ ✦

What was buried was never forgotten.

Chapter Eight

The Mark

The symbol appeared on Maya's hand before either of them realized the door had opened.

Elias stared at it.

He had seen the symbol before.

In a place where no living person should have seen it.

Chapter Nine

The Eyes in the Dark

The darkness moved.

Maya raised the light again.

This time there were more than two eyes.

Elias stepped backward.

✦ ✦ ✦

Whatever was waiting for them had finally noticed they were there.

Chapter Ten

The Monster Within Samarian

The name Samarian appeared across the ancient wall.

Maya recognized it from the tablet.

But the meaning was not what she expected.

The monster had not been locked away to keep it out.

It had been locked away to keep something else in.

Chapter Eleven

The Awakening Within

Maya finally understood why the tablet had called to her.

The ancient memory was not simply information.

It was a warning.

✦ ✦ ✦

And the warning had been waiting for someone to remember.

Chapter Twelve

The Door

The door opened without being touched.

Beyond it was not another chamber.

It was darkness filled with stars.

Elias looked at Maya.

Neither of them knew whether they were looking into the beginning of something—or its end.

Chapter Thirteen

The Presence

Maya felt it before she saw it.

A presence stood beyond the doorway.

Ancient.

Patient.

Waiting.

✦ ✦ ✦

Then the presence spoke.

And Maya realized the story had never really been about discovering the past.

It was about what the past had awakened.

Epilogue

Love Wins

Through the darkness.

Through the laughter.

Through the difficult chapters.

Even through the ridiculous Next Button.

✦ ✦ ✦
Love remembers.
Love forgives.
Love keeps going.
Love wins.
